# ScrollStamp — Reel Concepts & Scripts (landscape)

**The one line everything hangs off:**
> Bookmarks save the page. ScrollStamp saves the spot.

Never say "text anchoring," "six LLM platforms," "extension that bookmarks responses." Say **"you lose the thing you found."** Everyone has felt that. Nobody has felt "semantic anchoring."

**Rule for every script below:** the hook must work with the sound off, in 2 seconds. If the first frame needs explaining, it's dead.

---

## Concept 1 — "The Scroll of Shame" (no voiceover, best first post)

The pitch is the contrast. You don't explain anything — you just show the pain, then delete it.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Full screen: a huge ChatGPT/Claude thread. Your hand on the trackpad, scrolling. Fast. | **"it's in here somewhere"** |
| 0:02–0:08 | Speed the scroll to 4x. Let it go absurdly long. Add a ticking clock or a rising whoosh. Scrollbar handle shrinking to nothing. | (no text — let it get uncomfortable) |
| 0:08–0:09 | Hard cut. Silence. | **"or."** |
| 0:09–0:13 | Same thread. Click ScrollStamp → instant jump, the exact paragraph highlights. Satisfying click SFX. | **"ScrollStamp"** |
| 0:13–0:16 | Cursor selects a sentence → Bookmark → done. Two clicks, real time. | **"save the spot, not the page"** |
| 0:16–0:18 | Logo + "Chrome Web Store — free" | **"free. and it never leaves your computer."** |

**Why it works:** zero jargon, zero claims. The audience does the reasoning themselves. Trending audio with a hard beat drop at 0:09 does 90% of the editing for you.

## Concept 2 — "Wait, where did it say that?" (talking head + screen, landscape split)

Landscape's actual advantage: you on the left, screen on the right, both readable at once.

**Script (word for word):**

> **[0:00, straight to camera, no intro]**
> "You know that thing where you ask AI something really good... and then three days later you need it again, and it's just... gone?"
>
> **[0:04 — right panel starts scrolling a long thread, hopelessly]**
> "It's in there. Somewhere. Under two hundred messages about something else entirely."
>
> **[0:10 — lean in]**
> "So I made a thing."
>
> **[0:12 — right panel: select text, click Bookmark]**
> "You highlight the part that mattered. You save it."
>
> **[0:16 — right panel: click the saved item, page flies to the exact spot and it lights up]**
> "And whenever you want it back — it takes you *to the sentence.* Not the top of the page. The sentence."
>
> **[0:22]**
> "It's called ScrollStamp. It's free, it works on any website, and nothing you save ever leaves your computer."
>
> **[0:27 — beat, half smile]**
> "Bookmarks save the page. This saves the spot."

**Shot notes:** keep the screen recording sped up 1.5x behind you so it never lags the sentence you're on. The jump at 0:16 must play at real speed — that's the money shot, don't rush it.

## Concept 3 — "Name the person, not the use case" (rapid-fire, highest shareability)

Fast cuts, one line per person, each one a different real human. This is the one people tag their friends in.

**Format:** quick cut per beat, same framing each time, punchy audio.

> **[0:00]** "Bookmarks are broken and nobody talks about it."
>
> **[0:03]** "You asked AI to explain something at 2am and finally *got it* — that one paragraph." *(show: paragraph highlighting)*
>
> **[0:07]** "You planned a whole trip in one chat and now you can't find the day-three plan." *(show: long chat scrolling)*
>
> **[0:11]** "You're 9,000 words into documentation and you close the tab." *(show: docs page, tab closing)*
>
> **[0:14]** "You found the *one* line in a 40-page article that you'll want to quote." *(show: article, one line lit up)*
>
> **[0:18]** "Every bookmark you've ever made throws away the only thing you actually wanted. Where you were."
>
> **[0:23]** "ScrollStamp saves where you were." *(the jump + highlight, full screen, real speed)*
>
> **[0:27]** "Free. Any site. Stays on your machine."

**Why it works:** four different audiences see themselves in the first 15 seconds. You never once say what the software *is* — only what it gives back.

## Concept 4 — "The Highlighter" (metaphor-first, most "cool" visually)

Pure visual metaphor, almost no words. Good if you want the aesthetic post.

| Time | Shot | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Real world: a physical book, a real highlighter drawing across a line. Close-up, shallow depth of field, satisfying. | **"you can do this to a book"** |
| 0:03–0:06 | Snap-cut to a screen. Long AI chat. Cursor hovers uselessly. Nothing happens. | **"you can't do this to the internet"** |
| 0:06–0:08 | Beat. | **"until now"** |
| 0:08–0:14 | Screen: select → Bookmark → close tab → reopen → click → *fly to the exact line, highlight paints on.* One take, no cuts. | **"highlight it. come back to it. that's it."** |
| 0:14–0:18 | Logo. | **"ScrollStamp — bookmarks save the page. we save the spot."** |

**Shot notes:** the book shot is what makes this stop the scroll — real texture in a feed of screens. Match the highlighter color to your extension's highlight color so the cut lands as the *same action*.

## Production notes that apply to all five

**Format warning:** Reels are natively 9:16 and letterbox landscape footage into a small strip. If you're set on landscape, shoot **4:5 or 1:1 with a landscape screen-recording inset**, or crop landscape to 4:5 with center-safe framing. Full 16:9 in a Reel loses about 40% of the visual real estate to black bars and hurts reach. If the whole point is showing a wide browser window, put the browser in the top 60% and your face/captions in the bottom 40% — that's the layout that survives the vertical crop.

**The screen recording is the product.** Record it separately and obsessively:
- Use a *genuinely* long real conversation. A fake 12-message thread kills the credibility of the whole video.
- Zoom the browser to 125–150% before recording. Reel-sized text has to be huge.
- Speed up the scrolling pain. Never speed up the jump — the instant landing is the payoff and it needs to read as real.
- Add a soft SFX on the highlight paint. That single sound is what makes it feel like a product instead of a script.

**Never say in the voiceover:** extension, LLM, six platforms, anchoring, DOM, virtualized, restore. **Do say:** the spot, where you were, the sentence, lit up, comes right back.

**One-line closer options** (pick per video, don't stack them):
- "Bookmarks save the page. ScrollStamp saves the spot."
- "Save the sentence, not the site."
- "It remembers where you were, so you don't have to."

**Caption for the post:**
> Every bookmark you've ever made throws away the only thing you actually wanted — where you were.
> ScrollStamp saves the exact spot you were reading. Any website, any AI chat. Free, and nothing ever leaves your computer.
> Link in bio 🔖

**Comment-bait to pin:** "what's the thing you *know* you found online once and will never find again"

**Post order if you make more than one:** Concept 1 first (no-voice, algorithm-friendly, cheapest to make) → Concept 5 (funny, gets shared) → Concept 3 (broadest audience net) → Concept 2 (the real explainer, for people already curious) → Concept 4 (aesthetic, evergreen).
